chapter one

what this thing actually does📌

It gathers intel an analyst is allowed to have, pulls identifiers out of it, and shows how they connect. Then it tells you exactly how sure it is — and why.

🍓

Collect📌

Authorized feeds, research sets, analyst notes — each one keeps its source, timestamp and reliability tag.

🐚

Correlate

Handles, PGP keys, wallets, onion IDs and infra metadata get matched using weighted, tune-able evidence.

✿

Explain📌

Every score shows its receipts: what backed it, what argued against it, which evidence IDs it's built on.

what it flatly refuses to do

  • ✕ attack, scan or exploit hidden services or relays
  • ✕ try to break Tor anonymity or deanonymize ordinary users
  • ✕ identify private individuals from sensitive personal data
  • ✕ call an inference a confirmed real-world identity
chapter two

observations in, identifiers out

Drop in JSON, CSV or a STIX-like bundle. Watch each record get read and its identifiers lifted straight into the jar.

Normalised

Handles case-folded, wallets pattern-checked, addresses canonicalised before anything gets compared.

Traceable

Every entity keeps first-seen, last-seen, source count and the SHA-256 of the file it arrived in.

Rated📌

Sources are PRIMARY, SECONDARY, ANALYST or SYNTHETIC — none trusted just for wearing the label.
